{# Suggests other posts that share a tag or category with the current page. Shows nothing if there are none. Options (all optional): limit how many suggestions in total. Default 3. tags true / false: suggest posts that share a tag. Default true. category true / false: suggest posts that share a category. Default true. featured "prefer" (featured posts first, the default), "only" (featured posts only) or "ignore". heading the heading text. Default "Related content".
