I was nominated for and won September’s John Lewis Partnership One Partnership Award (OPA), an award to ‘recognise and celebrate Partners who are demonstrating the Partnership's Behaviours through a truly inspiring contribution.’

My contribution was the [storytelling](/tagging/storytelling) practice group called Presento Bento I set up for Partners in the Waitrose Online design and research team to build confidence and experiment with storytelling techniques in a safe to fail environment.

Huge thanks to Emma for nominating me in the first place, everyone who voted for me. A particular shout out to Jon, Mark, Zoe, Rob, Nat and Jason who joined Season 01.

Additional shout outs to various people in my career who I begged, borrowed and stole from to come up with the idea and shape the approach; Steph, Jeremy, Ben, Chris and Matt.
