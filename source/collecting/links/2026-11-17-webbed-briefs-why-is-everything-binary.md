Bangin’ ender.

> …those who create websites and web applications are divided into non-technical ideators and technical implementors. Organizationally speaking, this creates two departments:
> 
> 1. For thinking (but not about how to do the thing you are thinking about doing) and
> 2. For doing (but not thinking about why the hell anyone would think this could be done)
>
> …this setup is fundamentally why it sucks so badly. It’s not the binary in your computer at fault; it’s the binary we ascribe to our brains.
