{% pullquote "Joe Crawford – What’s the point?", "https://artlung.com/blog/2026/11/20/whats-the-point/" %}
I think of it as collecting more and more clay with which to make future sculptures.

Perhaps “a website is a machine to think.”

Or maybe “a website is a tool for thinking.”
{% endpullquote %}

Having just tinkered with the structure and design of my website, I think Joe’s onto somethings with this. Pouring over past content and imagining how existing and new content types work together has felt like returning to a canvas and seeing things from a new futures perspective.

I’ve written about the [wilderness of writing](/writing/2024/04/weeknotes-12-13/) before.

> My digital garden resembles the physical one outside my backdoor: imperfect, somewhat neglected but my own little place of sanctuary to grow my thoughts and play with ideas. Things are half finished or a work in progress but I’m fine with that. I’ll get around to some weeding weeding eventually or maybe even find time to plant a tree.

Most of all I subscribe to Joe’s position of a personal website as ‘tool for thinking’. Back to Joe’s metaphors of art, sculpture, workshops and workstations. I like to think of my website of a whetstone, something to sharpen my thinking and help me articulate the shapes of ideas in my head into a clear narrative.
